# Safety

The platform supports screening of visible/accessibly observable objects and environments. It cannot reliably detect narcotics inside a person's organs or diagnose ingestion. A missing visual detection is not evidence that a substance is absent.

No autonomous restraint, seizure, invasive examination, medical diagnosis, or enforcement decision is permitted.

Foundation and generative models are advisory only. Before motion, a deterministic gate must check emergency stop, localization confidence, collision clearance, human exclusion zones, workspace/joint limits, speed/force limits, actuator/battery health and communications watchdog state. Any failed check means STOP/HOLD and human intervention.

Keep UNSEEN, OCCLUDED, UNKNOWN and SUSPECTED_SUBSTANCE distinct. Use privacy, provenance, access control, retention limits and audit logs.
