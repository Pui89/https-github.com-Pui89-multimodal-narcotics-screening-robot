# Multimodal Screening Robot — cinematic 4D concept

**Status:** research concept storyboard only. Sensor capability, detection accuracy and operational legality are not established by this visualization.

## 30-second sequence
1. **0–5 s — Facility context:** polished airport cargo or logistics inspection lane, clean lighting and calm professional atmosphere.
2. **5–10 s — Robot reveal:** slow 180° orbit around a compact silver-and-navy mobile robot with RGB/depth cameras and clearly labeled modular sensor bay.
3. **10–17 s — Multimodal visualization:** a sealed test parcel moves through a controlled demonstration station; overlays visualize camera/depth alignment and generic sensor channels. Do not show a real substance or claim chemical identification.
4. **17–24 s — Decision support:** console displays “review required”, source-data trace, uncertainty indicator and audit log. A trained human operator reviews the flagged item.
5. **24–30 s — Responsible deployment:** robot remains stationary while the operator confirms the next step; end card: “PUI89 Multimodal Screening — human-in-the-loop research concept”.

## Video-generation prompt
Create a photorealistic cinematic 3D concept film, 16:9, 4K look, 24 fps, 30 seconds, in a clean modern inspection facility. Show a compact silver-and-deep-navy mobile robotics platform with RGB and depth cameras, modular sensor bay and a tablet/console interface. Use physically realistic materials, soft industrial lighting, slow camera dolly and orbit shots, precise industrial design details and consistent robot proportions. Demonstrate a sealed benign test parcel, abstract synchronized sensor streams, uncertainty visualization, audit trail and a clear “HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED” screen. Do not depict real narcotics, covert surveillance, automatic guilt judgments or unvalidated chemical sensing. End with “PUI89 Multimodal Screening — research concept”. Treat all sensor graphics as illustrative, not measured output.

## Suggested open-source workflow
- Blender for modeling, animation and rendering.
- FreeCAD for modular mounts and enclosure design.
- ROS 2 + Gazebo for simulated navigation and sensor integration.
- OpenCV for vision prototyping; use documented, lawful datasets.
- Kdenlive for final edit and captions.
